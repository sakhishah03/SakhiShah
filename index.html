Selected Work

Case studies

Three projects, real stakeholders, measurable outcomes.

the brief

A clinic with no marketing budget and no agency support needed to reach patients in the language they were actually comfortable in - without content that felt like it was performing for an audience that wasn't there.

the work

Owned the full content pipeline solo across Instagram and Facebook - strategy, copywriting, photography, design. Wrote and produced a 20-part prevention-focused Reels series averaging roughly 10K views a reel, using ChatGPT to calibrate scripts to the clinic's tone, Canva AI and CapCut for design and editing, and Veed.io to subtitle Gujarati-language audio in English. Layered in a 7-day Meta Ads Manager campaign across Western India and on-page SEO built around real search terms like "oncologist near me" - growing Instagram reach 129% in eight months, and, more tellingly, watching patients start sharing the content into their own networks unprompted.

the brief

What people actually say about a seasoning brand online rarely matches what a company assumes they're saying - the gap between the two was the real brief.

the work

A Brand24 sentiment analysis across 10,000+ posts became the basis for share-of-voice and engagement dashboards in Excel, and eventually an influencer strategy report for McCormick's industry advisors - flagging flavor-level content opportunities on TikTok that weren't obvious from the top-line numbers alone.

the brief

Willingness to pay is rarely what people say it is when asked directly - the harder question was designing research that could get past that.

the work
